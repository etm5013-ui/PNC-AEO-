How LLM retrieval works

Six steps between a prompt and a PNC mention

Answer engines don’t rank pages the way search does. They break a prompt into many searches, read a small set of passages, and decide which brands the evidence supports. Each step has its own failure mode and its own AEO lever.

AEO playbook for PNC

Four levers that move PNC into the answer

Each recommendation in the simulator maps to one of these levers. Most visibility gaps come from a few patterns: pages models can’t read, facts they can’t extract, and third-party sources that don’t mention PNC.

Content

Be the extractable answer

  • Open with a 40 to 60 word direct answer, then the details.
  • Write in the persona’s words (“no surprise fees,” “first paycheck”), not only product names.
  • Publish fair head-to-heads and worked examples (FHA vs. Community Loan at 5% down).
  • One hub page per journey (first-time buyer, first job, growing wealth).
Technical

Be readable by every retriever

  • Server-render rates, fees, benefit tables and tools. Most AI fetchers don’t run JavaScript.
  • Allow OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, Bingbot and Googlebot. Decide on training bots separately.
  • Use FAQPage, FinancialProduct, Offer and LocalBusiness schema with dateModified.
  • Push changes with IndexNow (Bing/Copilot) and keep sitemaps fresh.
Off-site

Be in the sources models trust

  • Publisher roundups (NerdWallet, Bankrate, Forbes Advisor, CNBC Select) set most shortlists.
  • Authentic presence in Reddit and YouTube communities, especially for ChatGPT and Gemini.
  • Complete Google Business Profile and Bing Places listings for branches, loan officers and advisors.
  • Expert quotes from PNC leaders inside authority content.
Measurement

Track the answer, not just the click

  • Run a fixed prompt panel by persona × intent × model every month.
  • Score mention rate, position, citation share and factual accuracy of PNC facts.
  • Segment AI referrers (chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com, claude.ai) in analytics.
  • Route inaccurate answers to Product and Compliance owners.
