How LLM retrieval works

Seven steps between a prompt and a PNC mention

Answer engines don’t rank pages the way search does. They break a prompt into many searches, read a small set of passages, and decide which brands the evidence supports. Each step has its own failure mode and its own AEO lever.

AEO playbook for PNC

Four levers that move PNC into the answer

Each recommendation in the simulator maps to one of these levers. Most visibility gaps come from a few patterns: pages models can’t read, facts they can’t extract, and third-party sources that don’t mention PNC.

Technical · Schema

Make every fact machine-readable

  • Roll out page-level JSON-LD: FinancialProduct (BankAccount, CreditCard, MortgageLoan), FAQPage, Article with audience, and dateModified.
  • Schema in the static HTML survives JavaScript rendering, so ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot can read it.
  • Gemini and AI Overviews get the biggest lift. Every model gets more accurate PNC facts.
  • Allow OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, Bingbot and Googlebot. Push changes with IndexNow.
Content · Personas

Write for the person asking

  • Pair each general SEO article with persona guides: students, starters, home buyers, emerging affluent and small business.
  • Persona pages match the persona sub-queries models fan out (“…for college students”), which general pages rarely win.
  • Open with a 40 to 60 word direct answer in the persona’s words, then the details.
  • Label the audience in schema so the match is explicit.
Off-site · Affiliates & social

Be named where models look

  • Models read affiliate roundups (NerdWallet, Bankrate, Forbes Advisor, CNBC Select) and count which brands they list, and in what position.
  • Reddit threads and YouTube transcripts add social proof. Sentiment matters.
  • ChatGPT weights these most, Claude least. AI Overviews pull them in when they rank.
  • Brief publishers with accurate product data, support creators, and join communities with disclosed, helpful answers.
SEO · Measurement

Rank, then track the answer

  • AI Overviews sit on the Google results page and cite mostly page-one results, so classic SEO is still the entry ticket.
  • Run a fixed prompt panel by topic × persona × intent × model every month.
  • Score mention rate, position, citation share and PNC fact accuracy.
  • Segment AI referrers (chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com, claude.ai) in analytics.
